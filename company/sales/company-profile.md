back-office operations — ERP, customer support, and reporting — delivering

## Medium (メール・提案書用 / EN)

Clearline Automation helps enterprises eliminate manual back-office work.
We automate one critical process at a time — SAP data entry and reporting,
Zendesk ticket handling, invoice processing, recurring reports — and deliver
it to production in 14 business days at a fixed price. Every delivery includes
human-in-the-loop approvals, audit logs, rollback procedures, and a handover
runbook. Founded by a bilingual (EN/JA) practitioner with hands-on experience
in SAP FI/S4HANA, Zendesk AI, Power Automate, and Python — and years inside
the back offices of Japanese enterprises in New York.
