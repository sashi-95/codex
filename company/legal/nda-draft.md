# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
{{日付}} ("Effective Date") by and between:

- Clearline Automation LLC, a {{州}} limited liability company ("Provider"), and
- {{顧客法人名}}, a {{州/国}} {{法人形態}} ("Client")

(each a "Party," together the "Parties").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning
back-office automation services (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential
information to each other in connection with the Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by
either Party, in any form, including business processes, financial data,
system configurations, credentials, personal data, and the existence and
terms of discussions between the Parties.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes
publicly available through no fault of the recipient; (b) was known to the
recipient before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of
the discloser's information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party
without confidentiality obligations.

## 3. Obligations

The recipient shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) protect it with at least the same degree of care used for its own
confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (c) not disclose
it to third parties except to employees, advisors, and subcontractors who
need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations
at least as protective as this Agreement.

## 4. AI/ML Processing (本事業固有の条項)

Recipient shall not input the discloser's Confidential Information into any
third-party AI/ML service **except** as expressly agreed in writing
(including in a Statement of Work), and in such case only via services
configured so the information is not used to train third-party models.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

法令・裁判所命令による開示は、可能な範囲で事前通知の上、必要最小限で行える。

## 6. Term

本契約は Effective Date から{{2}}年間有効。守秘義務は開示から{{3}}年間
(個人情報・認証情報は無期限) 存続する。

## 7. Return or Destruction

要求があった場合、受領者は Confidential Information を返却または削除し、
書面で確認する (法定保存義務・バックアップの残存は例外とし守秘義務は継続)。

## 8. No License / No Obligation

本契約は知的財産のライセンスを付与せず、取引の成立を義務づけない。

## 9. Remedies / Governing Law

違反は回復困難な損害を生じ得るため、差止救済を求めることができる。
準拠法: State of New York。管轄: {{New York County}}の州・連邦裁判所。
